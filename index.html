How it works

Each check measures your reaction time and how long you survive the dodge game (based on the classic "Pilot Trainer" shape game).

You first calibrate by doing at least 3 checks while rested and sober. Later checks are compared to that personal baseline, so a slow-but-steady older driver isn't judged against a 20-year-old.

Your score sets a speed cap below the posted limit. At your normal level the cap is 5 mph under the limit. Sharper than normal: no reduction. Slower than normal: a bigger reduction, down to "don't drive."

Drive mode reads your GPS speed and looks up the road's posted limit from OpenStreetMap, then warns you when you go over your cap. It cannot control your car.

Important

This app cannot detect alcohol or drugs and is not evidence of fitness to drive. If you've been drinking or using, or you feel off, don't drive — no matter what the score says. The tool is built so it can tell you to not drive, but it can never clear you to drive.

Drive mode sends your approximate location to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service to look up speed limits. Everything else stays on your phone.
